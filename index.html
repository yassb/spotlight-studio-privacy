Spotlight Studio by Damon Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Lesson playback and local data

The Microsoft Store edition includes lesson recordings that play offline. It does not require an account for the included library and does not use advertising or analytics. The app saves playback progress, listening history, lesson names, track assignments, and preferences on your computer. The bundled library is played through a local connection within your computer. Damon does not receive your playback history or preferences.

Support and downloads

If you choose Contact Damon, Spotlight Studio opens your email application. If you send a message, Damon receives the email address and information you include and uses it to respond and handle your support request. Your email provider handles delivery under its own policy. Microsoft handles Store acquisition and updates under its own privacy policy.

Visiting this policy page

GitHub Pages hosts this page. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security as described in the GitHub privacy statement. This page has no forms, ads, or analytics scripts.

Your choices

You can clear locally saved app data using Windows app settings where available. For privacy questions or to request deletion of information you sent for support, contact damon.studioapp@gmail.com.
